01 — Overview
Most AI tools wait for a prompt. Agent SK keeps working when you close the laptop.

Agent SK is an operating system for autonomous AI agents, designed around one obsession: financial markets. It ingests price action, order flow, macro releases, and news across asset classes — then decides, on its own, what deserves attention.

Every observation becomes memory. Every memory shapes the next decision. The result is an agent whose judgment compounds over time — the longer it runs, the more experienced it becomes.

02 — The learning loop
i.

Observe

Continuous ingestion of market data and events — tickers, filings, funding rates, macro calendars — filtered for what actually matters.

ii.

Remember

Events, outcomes, and its own past decisions are written to long-term memory. Nothing experienced is ever thrown away.

iii.

Adapt

Strategies are re-weighted against historical analogues and realized results. The agent revises its own playbook — no redeploy required.

iv.

Act

From morning briefs to configurable trading bots, actions are executed within hard risk rules the owner defines — autonomy, bounded by design.

No prompt required. The loop runs on its own schedule — 24/7.
